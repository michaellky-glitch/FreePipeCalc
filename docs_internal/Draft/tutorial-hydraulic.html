Tutorial — Hydronic — Hydraulic

How to use the HYDRAULIC tab. Top ribbon reference, then fluid, method, schedule, fittings, and limits.

Hydronic discipline · HYDRAULIC tab · v0.18.2

HYDRAULIC sets how friction is calculated. It controls fluid, friction method, pipe sizes, fitting losses, and warning limits. Change here = new result on the sheet.

NOTE:

Click HYDRAULIC in the top bar. The drawing hides. The hydraulic panel appears. Top ribbon is hidden here — mock-ups below show it for reference.

1. Overview

HYDRAULIC has five blocks, top to bottom:

  1. Fluid Properties — density, viscosity, specific heat.
  2. System — open or closed loop (auto-detected, read-only).
  3. Hydraulic Parameters — method and formula. Editable coefficients.
  4. Pipe schedule — bore and outside diameter per size.
  5. Fittings & Limits — equivalent length or K table, velocity and PD/m limits.

4. Fluid Properties

Fluid Properties — mock panel

Fluid: Water (preset)Custom → editable
Density ρ = 998 kg/m³Read-only for presets. Live: all Pa values use it.
Viscosity ν = 1.004e-6 m²/sSets Reynolds number and friction factor (Darcy).
Specific heat Cp = 4187 J/kg·KUsed on THERMAL tab: Q = ṁ·Cp·ΔT.

Fluid — what to do:

  • Select a preset. Use Custom to type your own values.
  • Named fluids are read-only. This keeps the sheet honest — the fluid name stays true to the numbers.
  • Density affects every pressure on the sheet. Change it and head-to-pressure changes.
  • Viscosity affects Darcy-Weisbach and the laminar check on both methods.
  • Temperature field is for record only. It does not recompute properties. Enter the temperature your data is for.

5. Hydraulic Parameters

Hydraulic Parameters — mock

Method: Hazen-Williams (default) or Darcy-Weisbach (BETA). Formula shows editable constants K, a, e.

Δh = K · L · (V/C)a · (1/D)e

Sheet records the method you used.

  • Hazen-Williams — For water, turbulent flow. Default. Uses C factor. Fittings as equivalent length.
  • Darcy-Weisbach — For any fluid, any regime. Uses roughness ε. Fittings as K. Correlation: Swamee-Jain (default), Haaland, Churchill, Colebrook-White.
  • Coefficients K, a, e are editable (ASHRAE Ch 22 Eq 6: 6.819, 1.852, 1.167). The solver derives flow-form coefficients from them.
  • BETA note on Darcy sheets — verify before you issue.

6. Pipe schedule

Pipe schedule — mock

Current schedule: e.g., Sch 40. Table shows size label → bore ID, outside diameter OD, insulation.

Buttons: Add Custom Schedule | Copy Current | Edit sizes | Delete (custom only).

  • Choose the schedule that matches your pipe. Bore sets velocity and loss.
  • Copy a standard schedule to make a custom one, then edit sizes.
  • New pipes use the last used size for that schedule. Change size on the pipe panel to override.

7. Fittings

Fittings — mock

Hazen-Williams → equivalent length table (Carrier default, NFPA 13 option, Custom editable). Darcy → K table (threaded default, flanged option).

Tables are keyed by nominal size, not bore. Between sizes: linear interpolation. Outside range: clamped.

  • You do not place fittings. The app finds them from geometry: elbows by angle, tees by flow split.
  • Tee leg (run vs branch) depends on flow. The app solves, re-classifies, solves again.
  • Published tables are read-only. Switch to Custom to edit.
  • Valves are not in this table. Valves use Kv/Cv (see pipe/valve panel).

8. Limits (warnings)

Warning thresholds — mock

Velocity limit (default 2.4 m/s) · Friction rate limit (default 400 Pa/m) · Laminar check on/off · Valve oversize · Heat balance · Max component PD.

Over limit = warning on sheet and status chip. Edit limits to suit your code.

  • Velocity — Colour and warning when V exceeds limit. High V means noise and erosion risk.
  • PD/m — Friction rate. Key sizing rule (~400 Pa/m). Warning when over.
  • Laminar (Re < 2300) — Warns when Hazen-Williams is out of its range. Switch to Darcy for laminar cases.
  • All limits are editable. The sheet checks against what you set.
SUGGESTION:

Set fluid and method first, then draw. Changing method after you draw recalculates everything. The sheet will show a different pump duty — that is correct, not an error.
